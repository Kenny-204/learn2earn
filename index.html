The Need for Learn2Earn Lagos

Nigeria has one of the highest youth unemployment rates in the world. Learn2Earn Lagos is a bet that the opportunity young Lagosians risk everything to find elsewhere can be built right here.

42.5%

FAQs

Who can apply?+

Anyone aged 18–35 who lives in Lagos, has at least an HND or Bachelor's degree, a valid LASSRA ID, basic computer skills, and is ready to commit to the full-time programme. Women and persons with disabilities are strongly encouraged to apply.

How long is the programme?+

The programme runs for 12 weeks of physical, full-time training, Monday to Friday.

Is there a certification?+

Yes. The programme leads to globally recognised certifications delivered through our certification partners.

Is this Free?+

Yes. The programme is 100% sponsored — training, laptops, transportation, meals, and a monthly stipend are all provided.

What is the application deadline?+

Applications close on 30th September 2026.
